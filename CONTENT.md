## AI Tools section
Intro: Infrastructure built at Cascade — brand, comms, ops and community, all systematised. Systems that let the team operate without me in the room.
Footer line: 6 systems · more in progress.
Categories: All / AI Content / Brand Infrastructure / Ops / Community / Analytics

1. [AI Content] Claude content project — tags: Claude
   Blurb: Brand voice, Cascade comms, and marketing copy — all runnable by the team without Lucas's input.
   How it works: A Claude Project (Anthropic's persistent context workspace) pre-loaded with Cascade's brand voice, tone guidelines, and example marketing copy. Anyone on the team opens the project, describes what they need in plain language, and gets on-brand output — no briefing required.
2. [Brand Infrastructure] Brand guidelines + tone of voice — tags: Notion, Figma
   Blurb: Written standards for visual identity, copy tone, and brand application — so the standard held without Lucas in the room.
   How it works: A three-part Notion doc covering (1) visual identity standards, (2) copy tone and voice guidelines, and (3) a content examples bank. Built as a resource for scanning all outgoing content to ensure branding accuracy — so any team member could QA their own work without a review cycle.
3. [Ops] VIP relationship database — tags: Notion
   Blurb: Depositor history treated as relationship capital — context, preferences, and conversation history in one place.
   How it works: A Notion database with one row per major depositor. Fields: deposit history, preferred comms channel, conversation log, last-touch date, next action. Treated less like a CRM and more like a relationship journal — the goal was to make every interaction feel personal even at scale.
4. [Ops] Daily marketing ops — tags: Claude, Notion, Granola, Slack
   Blurb: Pulls Granola, Slack, and Calendar into a structured Notion daily page. One command, full day context.
   How it works: A Claude skill (a structured prompt chain) that pulls from three sources: Granola meeting transcripts, Slack thread summaries, and the day's calendar. One command generates a structured Notion daily page with meeting prep, outstanding threads, and a task list. Turns context-gathering from a 20-minute morning ritual into a 30-second command.
5. [Analytics] KOL tracking dashboard — tags: Claude, Data
   Blurb: Visualising the referral networks of each KOL in the userbase — referred trading volume and invite code usage in one view.
   How it works: Built using direct user stats pulled from the platform. A dashboard that maps each KOL's referral network — showing referred trading volume, invite code usage, and campaign performance over time. Used to monitor influencer campaigns and allocate budget more effectively. Vibe-coded with Claude against our internal data exports.
6. [Community] Discord gated access system — tags: Discord, Claude
   Blurb: Community-access system tying Discord permissions and progression to specific platform activity — trading volume, deposits, and PnL.
   How it works: A permission system connecting Discord roles to on-chain and platform data. Users were automatically sorted into tiers based on trading enablement status, volume stats for VIP channel access, and PnL-based rankings. Built to make the community feel earned and exclusive without manual management overhead. Designed the logic, drafted the PRD, and worked directly with engineering to ship it.
